Hunting Game 1.2: Squad Up. Getting your group into a match is now much easier.

INVITE ANYONE IN SECONDS
- Every lobby has a QR code and an invite link. Friends scan it with their camera or tap the link, and they're in the lobby.
- No app yet? The link shows them where to get it and the code to type in.

REMATCH
- One tap after a match starts the next one with the same group, settings and play area.
- Everyone gets an invite and starts in the role they had last time.

QUICK SETUP
- Pick Small Group, Party or Quick Game and the match is set up for you, including a play area around where you're standing.
- Or lay down a 150, 300 or 500 m circle around you instead of drawing the area point by point.

ALSO NEW
- How to Play: a short walkthrough of the radar, catches, hearts, jail and power-ups.
- Share your result: a story-ready card for Instagram, Snapchat or Messages.
- Reports can include up to three screenshots as evidence.
- New Recruiter badge: host the first match of three new players.
- Bug fixes and improvements.
