Hunting Game 1.1 is our biggest update yet.

MAJOR UPDATES

Liquid Glass design
• The whole app is rebuilt on Apple's Liquid Glass on iOS 26 — tab bar, match HUD, cards and buttons — keeping the tactical look you know.
• A new app icon made of real Liquid Glass, with dark, clear and tinted styles for your home screen.
• Redesigned screens: a leaderboard podium with your own rank always pinned at the bottom, a new profile with achievement progress rings, and cleaner friends, lobby and roster lists.
• Much better accessibility: text grows to over 200% with your iPhone's text size, stronger contrast that rises further with Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion support, and VoiceOver labels everywhere — the radar even reads out where the nearest player is.

Power-ups, rebuilt
• Every power-up takes effect the moment you use it and ends exactly on time.
• Thermal Vision shows every runner within 300 m on your map as a heat signature, invisible runners included.
• Ghost Decoy sends a fake copy of you walking away from where you were heading — hunters can't tell it from the real you until they reach it.
• EMP Jammer knocks out the radar of every hunter within 200 m for 60 seconds.
• Invisibility hides you from the other side's radar or compass for 60 seconds.
• You only see and pick up the power-ups your side can use.

New ways to play
• Radar ping: hosts can set the radar to live, or to ping anywhere from every 10 seconds to every 10 minutes. Between pings, radar and compass go quiet — and Thermal Vision keeps tracking live.
• Runner head start: runners scatter first while the hunters' radar stays dark.
• Hearts are now lives in every mode: each catch costs a heart, and you keep playing until your last one.
• Answer catch requests straight from your lock screen.
• A match summary after every game: who caught whom, distance run, power-ups used, highlights and a timeline.
• The shrinking zone now scales to the length of your match.
• Customize: map styles, radar skins, map markers, profile banners and badges. Unlock looks by playing, or support the game with optional packs. Looks only — nothing for sale changes who wins.

Fairer matches
• No answer to a catch within 20 seconds counts as caught.
• Disagree about a catch? The hunter can insist, and the host decides.
• Runners freed from jail get 30 seconds before they can be caught again.
• The jail never costs hearts, and Safe Zone Flares no longer work there.
• Infection ends as soon as everyone has been turned.
• Squad mode now has a real winner: the last squad with a runner still free.

MINOR UPDATES
• Many bug fixes and smaller improvements throughout.
