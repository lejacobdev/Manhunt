Contact us

Email contact@lejacob.dev for anything — a bug, a question about your account, a report you want followed up, or something that just doesn't seem right. A real person reads every message and replies from the same address. If you're reporting a safety issue involving another player, include their username and tag (for example name#1234) so we can find their account quickly.

Signing in

I can't sign in.

In Login mode, enter your username and the 4-digit tag after the # in their separate fields. If you've forgotten your tag, it's on your Profile on any device where you're still signed in. Otherwise email us your username and we'll help — accounts have no email address, so we can't send a reset link.

Can I sign in with Apple or Game Center?

Yes, on the sign-in screen. If you already have an account, add either under Profile → Name & Sign-In, and from then on you can sign in with a tap. We never receive your email address from Apple. You can unlink them there too — unless one is the only way into your account, in which case set a password first.

Can I change my username or tag?

Yes — Profile → Name & Sign-In. You can change your name, your tag, or both, once every 7 days. If every tag on the name you want is taken, tap Random. Your friends stay your friends: they follow the account, not the name.

During a match

My radar or compass isn't moving.

Check the top of the screen: if the host set a radar ping, the radar only updates on each ping and shows "NEXT PING". Otherwise make sure Hunting Game has location access set to While Using or Always with Precise Location on, in Settings → Hunting Game → Location. An EMP from a nearby runner also blanks a hunter's radar for 60 seconds.

A runner says I didn't catch them.

When a runner says no, tap I did catch them and the host decides. If the host is one of you, or doesn't decide within 45 seconds, the catch stands.

How do power-ups work?

Every power-up is explained in the game rules.

Safety

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile — from Friends, a match roster or search — and use the ••• menu in the top corner. Reporting walks you through a category (cheating, harassment, an inappropriate username and so on) so we know exactly what happened. Blocking is immediate and removes them from your friends and your matches. Every report is reviewed by a person.

Hunting Game is played outdoors in real places. Follow traffic laws, stay aware of your surroundings, and never enter private property, restricted areas or traffic to make a catch. If another player makes you feel unsafe, report and block them, then email us — safety reports come first.

Your account

How do I stop sharing with Game Center?

On the Profile tab, switch off Share my scores and achievements. New scores stop being sent immediately; anything already on Game Center is managed in Apple's Game Center settings.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account. It removes your account, friendships, invites and match history straight away.

How old do I have to be?

At least 13. Players under 18 should have a parent's or guardian's permission. If you're a parent and your child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll remove it.
