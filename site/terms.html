What Hunting Game is

Hunting Game is a location-based, real-world game where players run, hide, and search for each other using live GPS position, shared with other participants of the same match only while that match is active. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what's collected and how it's used.

Location sharing is your choice, every match

The app never shares your location on a map automatically. Before your position is ever visible to anyone, you're shown an explicit prompt at the start of each match and must actively confirm you want to share your location for that match. Declining means you can't take part in that match's live gameplay, since the game fundamentally depends on it, but nothing is shared unless you say yes, every single time.
