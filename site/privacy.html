Hunting Game ("the app", "we") is a location-based real-world game for iOS and Apple Watch. This page explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how long it keeps it. There is no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics, and no data broker in this app — everything described below exists to run the game you're actually playing.

What we collect

  • Account. A username and a 4-digit tag (e.g. jhunter#4471) and a hashed password. We do not require, or ask for, an email address to create an account.
  • Sign-in identifiers (optional). If you choose to sign in with Sign in with Apple or Game Center, we store the one identifier Apple gives us for that purpose — for Apple, the account identifier unique to this app; for Game Center, your team player ID. That is all: we deliberately request no name or email address from Apple, and Apple's Hide My Email is therefore never involved. The identifier only ever says "this is the same person as last time", and you can remove either one at any time under Profile → Name & Sign-In.
  • Location. While you're an active participant in a match, the app continuously reports your GPS position — including while backgrounded — so hunters and runners can see each other's live bearing and distance on the radar/compass, so catches can be verified by proximity, and so the shrinking play-area boundary can be enforced. Location is not collected outside of an active match.
  • Motion data. Used on-device to distinguish on-foot movement from vehicle travel, as an anti-cheat signal during a match.
  • Camera. Used only to scan a friend's QR code when adding them. No photo or video is captured, stored, or uploaded.
  • Gameplay data. Match history, scores, power-up usage, and a replay log (the location trail above, tied to that one match) so you and the people you played with can review a finished match afterwards.
  • Friends & invites. The friend requests, friendships, and lobby invites you send or receive through the app.
  • Purchases (optional). If you buy a cosmetic pack or Supporter, Apple handles the payment — we never see your card or Apple Account details. We store the purchase Apple confirms to us (which product, Apple's transaction ID and the date), linked to your account, so what you bought stays unlocked on every device and can be restored. A refund removes it.
  • Connection country. While the app is connected, our server reads the country that our network provider (Cloudflare) derives from your connection, to show our moderators a count of players online per country. It is held only in memory while you're connected, never stored, and never shown next to your name or account.
  • Push notification token. If you enable notifications, Apple issues a device token we use solely to deliver game-related alerts (an invite, a match starting) through Apple's Push Notification service.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We never sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • Location is never collected outside of a match you're actively participating in.

How data is shared

Your live position during a match is shared only with the other participants of that same match, and only as the game requires: a hunter's phone receives a runner's bearing and distance, never their coordinates — with one exception, the Thermal Vision power-up, which for 45 seconds shows the hunter who used it the position of runners within 300 m. Match hosts and designated spectators can see the full roster of their own match. Nothing is shared outside the match itself, other than what you choose to share with Apple's Game Center (below), and nothing is shared with any other third party.

Game Center (optional)

From version 1.0.2 the app can connect to Apple's Game Center. It only does so if you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, and you can switch it off at any time with Share my scores and achievements on the Profile tab. When it is on, the app sends Game Center your career totals (matches won, runners caught, matches played and minutes played) and your achievement progress. They appear on Game Center's leaderboards and profile under your Game Center name, which Apple controls, and are governed by Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Game Center identity, friends or any other Game Center data, and the app does not send Game Center anything from an Apple Account marked as a child's.

Data retention & deletion

Match history and its associated location trail are kept so you can review past matches, and can be cleared at any time from Profile → Match History → Clear History. Deleting your account removes your profile, friendships, and pending invites. Scores and achievements you already shared with Game Center are held by Apple, not by us, and are not removed by deleting your Hunting Game account; you can manage them in Game Center itself. Location logs tied to a finished match may be retained in de-identified form for a limited period for anti-cheat and abuse investigation before being purged.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text — we cannot see your password.

Children's privacy

Hunting Game is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete that account and its data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has an account, email contact@lejacob.dev and we will remove it.

Players under 18 should have the permission of a parent or guardian. Your live position is shared only with the other players in a match you have chosen to join, and only after you agree to it for that match.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, or request deletion of your account and associated data, by contacting us at privacy@lejacob.dev.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update the "last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify active users in-app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@lejacob.dev. See also our Legal Notice for the operator's full identity.
