How it plays

Three steps to a manhunt

Set it up in a minute, then put the phone in your pocket and run.

01

Draw the arena

The host draws the play area on the map — and a jail if they want one — picks the mode, the match length and how often the radar pings.

02

Split up and go

Friends join with a code. The host makes some of you hunters and the rest runners. Runners get a head start, then the hunt is on.

03

Last one free wins

Hunters win when every runner is caught. Runners win if anyone is still free when time runs out — or if every hunter runs out of hearts.

Power-ups

Six ways to turn the chase

Scattered across the real play area — walk to one to pick it up. You only see the ones your side can use.

Anyone

Invisibility

Vanish from the other side’s radar or compass for 60 seconds.

Hunters

Thermal Vision

For 45 seconds every runner within 300 m shows on your map as a heat signature — invisible ones too.

Runners

Ghost Decoy

A fake copy of you walks away from where you were heading. Hunters can’t tell it apart until they reach it.

Runners

EMP Jammer

Every hunter within 200 m loses their radar for 60 seconds.

Runners

Adrenaline

Two extra hearts, and a higher top speed for 90 seconds.

Runners

Safe Zone Flare

A 30 m bubble where you stand. Nobody can be caught inside it for 90 seconds.

Modes

Three ways to play

Standard

Hunters against runners. Turn on jail mode and caught runners walk to the jail, where a free teammate can break them out.

Infection

Every runner caught switches sides and joins the hunt. The match ends when nobody is left running.

Squad

Teams, each with hunters and runners. Chase the other squads, protect your own — the last squad with a runner free wins.

In the app

Real screens, straight from the app

On your wrist

The Apple Watch app shows your radar or compass, hearts and catch requests, and taps you when a hunter closes in — so your phone can stay in your pocket.

On your Lock Screen

A Live Activity and the Dynamic Island keep the nearest hunter’s distance and the match clock in view without opening the app.

Safety & privacy

Built for real places and real people

Location only in a match

Your position is shared only during a match you joined, after you agree to it for that match — and only as a bearing and distance, never a pin on someone’s map.

Report and block

Every player can be reported or blocked from their profile. Reports reach a real person, and blocking takes effect immediately.

13 and over

No ads, no tracking, no data brokers. Players under 18 should play with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. Always obey traffic laws and stay aware of your surroundings.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Hunting Game free?

Yes. Hunting Game is free to download and play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How many people do I need?

At least two: one hunter and one runner. It gets better with more — a few hunters against a bigger group of runners is a classic match, and Squad mode splits everyone into teams.

Where can I play?

Anywhere outdoors that’s public and safe to walk or run: a park, a campus, a quiet neighbourhood. The host draws the play area on the map before the match, and a zone inside it shrinks as the clock runs down.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The Watch app is a bonus: your radar or compass, hearts and catch requests on your wrist, so your phone can stay in your pocket.

Does it drain my battery?

Live GPS uses battery like any run-tracking app. A charged phone comfortably lasts a normal match, and location is only used while a match is running.

Can other players see where I am?

Not on a map. Hunters see runners as a bearing and distance on their radar, and runners see hunters on a compass — except for 45 seconds within 300 m of a hunter using Thermal Vision. Your location is only shared during a match you joined, after you agree to it for that match.

What happens when I get caught?

A hunter within about 15 m sends a catch request, and you answer it on your phone — even from the lock screen. Hearts are lives: each catch costs one. With jail mode on you walk to the jail and wait for a teammate to break you out; otherwise you get a 30-second shield to get away and keep playing. Lose your last heart and you’re out.

How old do I have to be?

Hunting Game is for players aged 13 and over. Players under 18 should have a parent’s or guardian’s permission.
